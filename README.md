# 📱 AIUBSports

## 💡 The Real-World Scenario
During my time on campus, I observed a significant logistical bottleneck in how sports facilities were managed. Students had to physically visit the sports office to check for gym or field availability, which often led to scheduling conflicts, wasted time, and manual errors. Additionally, tournament registration was a slow, paper-based process.

## ✅ The Solution
I developed **AIUBSports** to solve these friction points by digitizing the entire management workflow. The app creates a direct, real-time bridge between the Sports Office (Admin) and the Students, ensuring transparency and efficiency.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎓 Student Portal
* **🏟 Real-Time Slot Booking:** Instantly check facility availability. The system uses a **live sync** mechanism to turn slots "Red" (Booked) or "Green" (Available) instantly across all devices, preventing double-booking conflicts.
* **🏆 Digital Tournament Entry:** Browse upcoming events and register teams directly through the app, eliminating paper forms.
* **📰 Live News Feed:** Receive official sports announcements and schedule changes immediately.
* **👤 Verified Profile:** Secure access ensures only valid students can participate.

### 🛡️ Admin Portal
* **📊 Command Center:** A central dashboard to monitor all campus sports activities.
* **📅 Dynamic Inventory:** Create and manage time slots for Football, Cricket, and Gym facilities on the fly.
* **✅ Application Control:** Review, approve, or reject team applications with a single tap.
* **📢 Broadcast System:** Push news updates to all student devices simultaneously.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Language:** Kotlin
* **Architecture:** MVVM / MVC Pattern
* **Backend:** Firebase Realtime Database (for milliseconds-latency updates)
* **Authentication:** Firebase Auth (Secure Role-Based Login)
* **UI/UX:** Material Design, CardViews, Custom RecyclerView Adapters
